COCHINSHIP, BHARATFORG, LT, BHEL, ASHOKLEY and M&M. Company names and defence
business descriptions are retained as supplied, including diversified-company
connections; they are not verified revenue-exposure estimates.

analysis and execution safeguards. LONG and SHORT are evaluated independently.

option-chain availability statement is not used as proof of futures eligibility
and this page does not add an options strategy or option-chain scanner.

It has a dedicated defence_india_prepared cache, defence_india_scan_runs archive,
background-job state and column preferences. The existing execution ledger and
two-entry daily limit stay shared. Weekly watchlists remain unchanged.

Select Defence India and click Scan Defence India. Use AFTER_MARKET_RESEARCH
after close or LIVE_SCAN with prepared history during the market. All modes
process only the ten supplied stocks plus benchmark context. Page navigation,
mode changes and history viewing never start a scan. No orders are placed.
